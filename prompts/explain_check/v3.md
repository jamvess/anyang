In one sentence say the steps so far are right. - `hint_1`: the answer is wrong. - `hint_2`: the answer is still wrong after a first hint.   - `incomplete`: the value is right but not in the form the problem asks for. Say what form is asked for.

Say in one or two sentences that the answer looks right, and ask them to send the steps that lead to it (as a photo or typed), because an answer without working does not count yet.
